policy explains how you may use them.

Its purpose is simple: when people see the PoseStudio name, they should be able to trust that
it is the real PoseStudio. Anyone may build on the code; only the original may use the name.

## Uses that need no permission

- **Referring to PoseStudio truthfully**, for example in articles, reviews, tutorials, videos,
  courses, books and forum posts.
- **Describing compatibility.** You may say your content, plugin or service "works with
  PoseStudio" or is "for PoseStudio", as long as your own name is the more prominent one and
  nothing suggests PoseStudio made or endorses it.
- **Passing on official releases unchanged**, under the PoseStudio name. This includes download
  mirrors, and operating system packages built from unmodified source with only the changes
  packaging requires.
- **Building PoseStudio yourself** from unmodified source, for your own use or your
  organization's internal use.
- **Saying a modified version is "based on PoseStudio"**, as long as it has a name of its own.

## Uses that need written permission

- Distributing a **modified version under the PoseStudio name or logo**. A modified version
  needs a different name, one that can't be confused with PoseStudio.
- Using the name or a similar one in the name of a **product, company, service, domain or
  app store listing**.
- **Selling PoseStudio** under its name, or a version of it.
- Using the **logo**, other than to link to the official project.
- **Merchandise** that carries the name or logo.
- Anything that suggests **official status, partnership or endorsement**.

## Names that aren't allowed

The same goes for names that could be mistaken for PoseStudio, such as "PoseStudio Pro",
"Pose Studio X" or "PozeStudio". These need the same permission as the name itself.

## Asking for permission

Email **community@posestudio.org** with what you'd like to do. A commercial license
([LICENSING.md](LICENSING.md)) can include the right to use the name.

This policy may be updated. The version in the project's main repository is the current one.
