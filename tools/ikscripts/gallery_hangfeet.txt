# THE HANGING FEET COME DOWN FLAT (2026-11-12; the user: "when a character is lifted off the ground,
# her feet go into a hanging tip toe position; dropping back to the ground should also make their
# feet go back to flat on the ground position"). A lift leaves the feet rolled onto their balls (the
# lift-off's tiptoe) and pointed (the dangle), and let go of in the air she keeps them — rightly,
# while she hangs. Coming back DOWN they go flat again, however she comes down: the Ground button's
# fall, a hips drag that lowers her, a Ctrl carry. `tilt.<foot>` is the foot's tilt from level.

# 1. Lifted by the hips and let go of: the feet hang. The Ground button: she lands on flat feet.
view right
press hip
dragv 0 0.30 30 60
release
expect lowest > 100
expect tilt.lFoot > 20
view right
closeup lFoot 0.30
shot 701_hanging_feet
ground
wait 8
shot 702_falling_feet
wait 100
expect tilt.lFoot < 2
expect tilt.rFoot < 2
expect tilt.lToe < 2
expect meshlow > -2
expect meshlow < 2
view right
closeup lFoot 0.30
shot 703_grounded_feet
view home
shot 704_grounded

# ... and it is ONE pose edit: Undo puts her back in the air with her feet hanging, and dropped
# again she lands flat again (the hang is recognised by the pose itself, whatever came between).
undo
expect meshlow > 150
expect tilt.lFoot > 20
ground
wait 110
expect tilt.lFoot < 2
expect meshlow > -2
expect meshlow < 2
reset
