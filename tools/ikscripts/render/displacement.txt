# DISPLACEMENT IN A RENDER (scene/meshdisplace.h; the Materials tab's Displacement section).
#
# Needs a build with the render engine. NOT run by run_all.sh (the subfolder). On the six-sphere
# scene and the loose maps of tools/materials/make_test_assets.py:
#
#   python tools/materials/make_test_assets.py <assets>
#   POSESTUDIO_TEST_FIGURE=<assets>/showcase.gltf tools/ikscripts/run.sh \
#       tools/ikscripts/render/displacement.txt <out> POSESTUDIO_IK_SCRIPT_ASSETS=<assets> \
#       POSESTUDIO_RENDER_TRACE=1
# (POSESTUDIO_RENDER_TRACE prints what each displacement did: the levels, the triangles, how far
# the surface moved and how long it took.)
#
# The Chalk sphere (half a metre in radius) is given a displacement map and rendered: a zone
# with a map, a strength and its layer on is subdivided and MOVED on its way to the engine —
# real geometry, with a silhouette. render.covered is the share of the picture that is not the
# backdrop (the floor's shadows are off, so it is the spheres' silhouette): a surface displaced
# outward covers more, inward less. The gates are the numbers measured (plain 43.6, domes 44.4,
# blocks raised 47.0, sunk 42.9). LOOK at render/: 01 raised domes that stand out of the
# outline, 02 raised blocks with crisp sides, 03 the same blocks sunk, 04 what the sphere's own
# vertices alone make of the map, 05 the plain sphere again — and the two viewport shots: the
# Rendered mode shows the blocks, the PBR mode does not (the rasterized modes do not displace).

wait 10
light shadows 0
select 0
zone Chalk
lookat 2.75 0.5 0 0.62
wait 3

# --- 0. The plain sphere -------------------------------------------------------------------------
render 00_plain 48
expect render.ok == 1
renderref
expect render.covered > 42.6
expect render.covered < 44.6

# --- 1. Domes on a mid-grey map: the surface stands out where the map is brighter than the mid level
texture displacement overlay_bump.png
expect mattex.displacement == 2
material displacement 3
material displacementmid 50
material displacementsubd 4
expect mat.displacement == 3
expect mat.displacementsubd == 4
render 01_domes_displaced 48
expect render.ok == 1
# (The domes on the outline stand out of it: a little more of the picture is covered.)
expect render.covered > 44.1
expect render.fromref > 0.5

# --- 2. A black / white checker from a mid level of 0: the white squares rise 4 cm ----------------
texture displacement tex_mask.png
material displacement 4
material displacementmid 0
render 02_checker_raised 48
expect render.ok == 1
expect render.covered > 46

# --- 3. A negative strength sinks them instead ----------------------------------------------------
material displacement -4
expect mat.displacement == -4
render 03_checker_sunk 48
expect render.ok == 1
expect render.covered < 43.2

# --- 4. Without subdivision the mesh's own vertices are all there is to move ----------------------
material displacement 4
material displacementsubd 0
render 04_checker_no_subdivision 48
expect render.ok == 1
expect render.covered > 44.6

# --- 5. The layer switched off: the sphere as it was ----------------------------------------------
material displacementsubd 4
material displacementenabled 0
render 05_layer_off 48
expect render.ok == 1
expect render.covered > 43.3
expect render.covered < 43.9
expect render.fromref < 0.6
material displacementenabled 1

# --- 6. The Rendered viewport mode shows the displacement; the rasterized modes do not ------------
shade 1
tracedwait
expect traced.done == 1
shot 06_rendered_mode_displaced
shotref
shade 0
wait 3
shot 07_pbr_mode_not_displaced
expect shot.fromref > 1
