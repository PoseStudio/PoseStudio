# PICKING IS WHAT THE CURSOR IS ON (2026-09-26): a click is cast against the posed skin, the first
# surface wins, and a part hidden behind another is never selected through it. `click <bone>` clicks
# at the bone's joint's own pixel; `selected.<bone>` reads whether that bone (or its rigid segment)
# ended up selected.
#
# In `view right` the camera stands at +X. She faces +Z, so her LEFT side is the near one: clicks at
# the RIGHT shoulder's, hand's, thigh's and knee's pixels land on the left arm, the torso or the left
# leg, and must not select the hidden parts.
view right
click rShldr
expect selected.rShldr == 0
click rHand
expect selected.rHand == 0
click rThigh
expect selected.rThigh == 0
click rShin
expect selected.rShin == 0
# ... and what IS in front is selected: the left arm, the left leg.
click lShldr
expect selected.lShldr == 1
click lHand
expect selected.lHand == 1
click lShin
expect selected.lShin == 1
# A click on the side of the head is the head's skin (a face bone, promoted to the head by a drag),
# never an arm.
click head
expect selected.lShldr == 0
expect selected.rShldr == 0

# From the front a click on the chest is the chest — never the collar or shoulder joints inside and
# behind it — a knee is its knee, a hand its hand, and a click right on the hip joint's pixel is the
# HIP: the skin there is the pelvis bone's (the root has none of its own), and the snap takes the
# joint of the hit bone's PARENT when it stands under the cursor, not the pelvis joint 10px away.
view front
click chest_2
expect selected.lCollar == 0
expect selected.rCollar == 0
expect selected.lShldr == 0
expect selected.rShldr == 0
expect selected.hip == 0
click lShin
expect selected.lShin == 1
click lHand
expect selected.lHand == 1
click hip
expect selected.hip == 1
# From the back the same pixel is the sacrum, the pelvis bone's skin again: the hip.
view back
click hip
expect selected.hip == 1
# From the side her hanging hand covers the hip: the honest pick is the hand's finger there, and the
# hip is NOT selected through it (the galleries' `press hip` falls back to the name and says so).
view right
click hip
expect selected.hip == 0

# Esc, with nothing else claiming it, deselects whatever is selected: the model and its joint.
view front
click hip
expect selected.hip == 1
expect selection == 0
app esc
expect selection == -1
expect selected.hip == 0
