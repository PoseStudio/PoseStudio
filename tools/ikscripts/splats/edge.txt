# THE SILHOUETTE IN FRONT OF A CAPTURE (composite.frag, THE EDGE; photoresolve.frag): the scene is
# drawn multisampled and resolved, so a pixel a figure half covers is half her color and half the
# BACKDROP's; and the Gaussian splats, tested against the nearest of the pixel's depth samples,
# are kept out of that whole pixel. Laid together as they stood, a dark stair-stepped line of the
# viewport's grey ran around her wherever a capture stood behind her (a blocky fringe several
# pixels wide with the splat layer scaled down, as VR Mode scales it). The composite now puts
# such a pixel together from her own color and the capture behind her.
#
# NOT run by run_all.sh (the subfolder): it needs the test scene —
#   python tools/splat/make_test_scene.py <assets>/test_scene.ply
#   POSESTUDIO_TEST_FIGURE=<figure> tools/ikscripts/run.sh tools/ikscripts/splats/edge.txt <out> \
#       POSESTUDIO_IK_SCRIPT_ASSETS=<assets>
# and again with POSESTUDIO_SPLAT_SCALE=0.5 (the layer at half size: the scaled path, which is
# what a headset on a slower graphics card shows). No render engine is needed.
#
# THE CHECK: the top of her upper arm from the front, lit, with the capture's blue-grey wall behind
# it — everything in the frame is bright, skin or wall, so the darkest pixel of the shot says
# whether a line of the backdrop runs along her outline. Measured on the base figure: 126-134
# with the blend in the four modes below; 75-99 without it (POSESTUDIO_SPLAT_NO_EDGE=1), 61-64
# without it at half scale. The gate is 115.
import test_scene.ply

expect shot.darkest > 115

expect shot.darkest > 115
shade 4

expect shot.darkest > 115
shade 0
