# A CLICK PICKS WHAT THE CURSOR IS ON — between a figure and a Gaussian splat scene (Scene::pickModel,
# Model::pickExact, SplatCloud::pick; the joint pick's gate in Scene::pickBoneAt). Until 2026-11-12
# a click chose a model by its BOX — a figure's is her posed joints plus a flesh margin, as wide as
# her arms — so with a capture close behind her every click on the capture entered her box first
# and selected HER; a click on the capture near her outline took the joint that projects nearby;
# and a click on a capture standing in FRONT of her took her joint through it. Now the capture's
# own splats are cast: where they cover the pixel under the cursor, the click is on the capture.
#
# NOT run by run_all.sh (the subfolder): it needs the test scene —
#   python tools/splat/make_test_scene.py <assets>/test_scene.ply
#   POSESTUDIO_TEST_FIGURE=<figure> tools/ikscripts/run.sh tools/ikscripts/splats/pick.txt <out> \
#       POSESTUDIO_IK_SCRIPT_ASSETS=<assets>
# The scene is scaled to 40 % and set behind her, so that its wall — then 1.6 m wide and 0.8 m
# tall — stands 40 cm behind her legs, and the whole capture lies behind her box as a captured
# person standing behind her would. `clickat <x> <y> <z>` is a real left click at the pixel a
# world point is seen at; `selection` is the selected model (0 the figure, 1 the capture, -1
# none). With POSESTUDIO_PICK_MODEL_BOX=1 and POSESTUDIO_PICK_THROUGH=1 — the old pick — every
# click on the capture below selects the figure.
import test_scene.ply
wait 20
expect figures == 2
select 1
scale 40
move 0 0 -160
select -1
view front
lookat 0 0.9 0 1.3
shot 01_in_front_of_the_wall

# The wall beside her leg, well inside her box (her arms reach further out than this).
clickat 0.5 0.4 -0.4
expect selection == 1
shot 02_capture_selected
select -1
# The wall right beside her knee — within the joint pick's reach of it on screen.
clickat 0.24 0.5 -0.4
expect selection == 1
select -1
# ... and on her other side.
clickat -0.45 0.6 -0.4
expect selection == 1
select -1
# Her own chest and her thigh: hers, as ever.
clickat 0 1.28 0.1
expect selection == 0
select -1
clickat 0.09 0.72 0.08
expect selection == 0
shot 03_figure_selected
select -1
# Beside her, above the wall: nothing is there — but her box is, and a click just off a figure
# still selects her (the box is the fallback where the cursor is on no surface).
clickat 0.45 1.1 0
expect selection == 0
# Far off both: the selection is cleared.
clickat 1.6 1.9 0
expect selection == -1

# SHE STANDS BEHIND THE WALL: it hides her legs. A click on it there is on the capture, not on
# the knee behind it.
select 0
move 0 0 -80
select -1
shot 04_behind_the_wall
clickat 0.09 0.5 -0.4
expect selection == 1
select -1
# Her chest shows above it and is hers.
clickat 0 1.28 -0.7
expect selection == 0
shot 05_figure_selected_behind
