# Out and home again: the pose is a function of the cursor, so she must stand exactly as she
# stood — the body, the hanging arms and the righted head alike. And what a drag made must still
# be there when the button comes up. No shots but one: numbers.
view right

# One drag: hips back into the hinge, and home.
press hip
dragv 0.10 0 40 20
dragv -0.10 0 40 30
expect grab < 1
expect head < 2
# (the idle arm's ELBOW joint: its hand relaxes 5cm by design — IDLE ELBOWS SOFTEN, 2026-11-02)
expect moved.lForeArm < 3
expect gaze > -1
expect gaze < 1
release
reset

# One drag: a bow, and back up.
press chest@0.6
dragv -0.18 -0.08 50 20
expect gaze < -20
dragv 0.18 0.08 50 40
expect grab < 1
expect steps == 0
expect head < 3
expect moved.lForeArm < 5
expect gaze > -1.5
expect gaze < 1.5
release
reset

# TWO drags: a bow let go of, then the chest taken back up. The neck ends where it began and the
# arms are back at her sides — a hand at 1.08m (they once stuck straight out in front of her: 1.4m).
press chest@0.6
dragv -0.20 -0.10 50 30
release
expect tilt.chest > 40
expect y.lHand < 1000
press chest@0.6
dragv 0.20 0.10 50 40
expect grab < 3
expect gaze > -4
expect gaze < 4
expect y.lHand < 1200
expect tilt.chest < 6
shot two_drags_unbowed
release
reset

# LET GO OF MID-STEP: the chest dragged far enough forward to walk, the button up while a foot is
# in the air. The release settle may land the foot — it must not un-make the pose (the chest
# once sprang back 17cm and the head 44cm as the foot came down).
press chest@0.6
dragv -0.30 -0.10 40 4
release
expect tilt.chest > 35
expect gaze < -25
expect y.lHand < 1000
reset

# A head the user turned DOWN keeps its own pose over and above the righting: leaned, she looks
# further down than she did; brought back, exactly as far as she was posed.
fk head 20 0 0
press hip
dragv 0.10 0 40 30
expect gaze < -12
dragv -0.10 0 40 30
expect gaze < -17
expect gaze > -23
release
quit
