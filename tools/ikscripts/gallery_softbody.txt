# SOFT BREASTS (armaturebreasts.cpp): the breasts hang with the chest's orientation against
# gravity and bounce on a landing. The Posing preference is on by default, so the app runs this
# with it on; `breastl` / `breastr` read the swing in degrees. Standing they rest as sculpted;
# a bow hangs them (the sag toward the belly relieved: they lift toward the head and hang out);
# Reset Pose rests them again; the Ground button's landing bounces them and the arms, then rests.
view right
shot 01_standing
closeup chest 0.30
shot 01b_standing_closeup
view home
view right
expect breastl < 0.5
expect breastr < 0.5

# A bow by the chest: a static hang, there at the end of the settle as during the drag.
press chest@0.6
dragv -0.30 -0.25 50 40
shot 02_bow_drag
expect breastl > 5
expect breastr > 5
release
shot 03_bow_rest
expect breastl > 5
# ... and in close-up: the nipples lead, pointing at the floor, the breast's base where it was.
closeup chest 0.30
shot 03b_bow_closeup
reset
view home
view right
expect breastl < 0.5

# Lying back: the hips down onto the floor, then the chest reclined — the breasts lift toward the chin.
press hip
dragv 0 -0.80 60 30
release
press chest@0.6
dragv 0.45 -0.35 60 40
release
shot 04_reclined
expect breastl > 4
reset

# The landing: lifted half a metre and dropped, shot through the bounce, at rest afterwards.
view home
lift 0.5
ground
wait 24
shot 05_landing
wait 90
shot 06_landed
expect breastl < 0.5
expect lowest >= -5
