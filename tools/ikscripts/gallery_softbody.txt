# SOFT BREASTS (armaturebreasts.cpp): the breasts hang with the chest's orientation against
# gravity and bounce on a landing. The Posing preference is on by default, so the app runs this
# with it on; `breastl` / `breastr` read the swing in degrees, `breasthangl` / `breasthangr` the
# hang along the tether in mm. Standing they rest as sculpted; a bow swings them (the sag toward
# the belly relieved) and draws them out toward the floor, the nipple leading; Reset Pose rests
# them again; the Ground button's landing bounces them and the arms, then rests. The amounts are
# the BASE FIGURE'S (fullness 1: a large-busted character hangs twice as far, a modest one a
# quarter, a flat chest not at all), and so are the gates: run on another body, read the numbers.
view right
shot 01_standing
closeup chest 0.30
shot 01b_standing_closeup
view home
view right
expect breastl < 0.5
expect breastr < 0.5
expect breasthangl < 0.5

# A LEVEL-CHESTED BOW — the user's pose (the chest horizontal, a hand reaching for the floor): the
# breast hangs off the chest wall as a teardrop, the nipple at its lowest point pointing at the
# floor; looked at from the side, from three-quarters and from below.
press chest@0.6
dragv -0.17 -0.12 50 40
release
shot 02b_level_bow
expect breastl > 5
expect breasthangl > 15
expect breasthangr > 15
closeup chest 0.30
shot 02c_level_bow_closeup
orbit 40 -12
shot 02d_level_bow_threeq
orbit -80 0
shot 02e_level_bow_below
reset
view home
view right
expect breastl < 0.5
expect breasthangl < 0.5

# A deep bow by the chest: a static hang, there at the end of the settle as during the drag.
press chest@0.6
dragv -0.30 -0.25 50 40
shot 02_bow_drag
expect breastl > 10
expect breastr > 10
release
shot 03_bow_rest
expect breastl > 10
expect breasthangl > 10
# ... and in close-up: the nipples lead, pointing at the floor, the breast's base where it was.
closeup chest 0.30
shot 03b_bow_closeup
reset
view home
view right
expect breastl < 0.5

# Lying back: the hips down onto the floor, then the chest reclined — the breasts lift toward the
# chin (a swing; no hang into the chest: lying down gravity presses them, which is a morph's job).
press hip
dragv 0 -0.80 60 30
release
press chest@0.6
dragv 0.45 -0.35 60 40
release
shot 04_reclined
expect breastl > 3
expect breasthangl < 0.5
reset

# ON HER BACK (the hip rolled back 90 degrees, in the air: the floor is not the point) the breasts
# flatten a little — pressed back toward the chest wall, `breasthangl` reads NEGATIVE — and fall a
# little to the sides (`breastsidel` / `breastsider` + outward); ON HER SIDE they fall toward the
# lower side: the upper breast in toward the sternum, the lower one out. More on a fuller bust.
view home
fk hip -90 0 0
expect breasthangl < -4
expect breastsidel > 8
expect breastsider > 8
view top
closeup chest 0.34
shot 07_on_her_back_from_above
reset
view home
fk hip 0 0 90
expect breastsidel < -5
expect breastsider > 5
view top
closeup chest 0.34
shot 08_on_her_side_from_above
reset
view home

# The landing: lifted half a metre and dropped, shot through the bounce, at rest afterwards.
view home
lift 0.5
ground
wait 24
shot 05_landing
wait 90
shot 06_landed
expect breastl < 0.5
expect lowest >= -5

# SOFT BUTTOCKS (armaturebuttocks.cpp): the glutes' skin jiggles with the hips' movement at half
# the breasts' amounts and NEVER hangs — standing, bowed or lying they read 0 at rest (`buttl` /
# `buttr`, mm) — and a quick sideways drag of the hips sets them swaying; the settle rests them.
reset
expect buttl < 0.01
view back
closeup hip 0.40
press hip
dragv 0.10 0 6 10
expect buttl > 2.5
expect buttr > 2.5
shot 09_hips_jolt_back
release
expect buttl < 0.5
expect buttr < 0.5
reset
