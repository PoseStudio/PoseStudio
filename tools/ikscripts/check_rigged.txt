# A rigged character from an interchange file (FBX, glTF / GLB, USD) — or any figure: imported,
# shot from the front and the side, then posed once by the hand and once by the hips, so that the
# rig, the skinning and the full-body IK are seen to work on it. Numbers first, pictures for the
# eye. Run on a file: POSESTUDIO_TEST_FIGURE=<file> tools/ikscripts/run.sh tools/ikscripts/check_rigged.txt <out>
view front
shot 01_front
expect lowest >= -5
view right
shot 02_side

# The hand pulled forward and up: the hand on the cursor, the feet planted, the body following.
press lHand
dragv -0.20 0.20 40 30
shot 03_hand_pulled
expect grab < 30
expect lowest >= -10
release
expect moved.lFoot < 15
expect moved.rFoot < 15
reset

# The hips taken down: a crouch, the knees folding, nothing through the floor.
view home
press hip
dragv 0 -0.25 40 30
shot 04_crouch
expect hips > 150
expect lowest >= -10
expect moved.lFoot < 15
release
reset

# Reset Pose brings the figure back as imported.
view front
report after_reset
expect hips < 1
shot 05_reset
