# CTRL + DRAG (2026-09-26/28, the torso 2026-10-28). On a limb, the head or the TORSO it is SCOPED:
# the grabbed chain alone follows the cursor — a hand pulled forward moves its arm and nothing else,
# the head bends the neck, the chest bends the spine down to the pelvis, a knee swings over its
# planted foot — and the numbers a user would see are what did NOT move: the hips, the feet, the
# chest, the other hand — 0.0mm, since a bone none of the solve's unknowns move does not move at
# all. On the HIPS it MOVES THE FIGURE as she is posed, off the floor's contacts, the user's pins
# holding.
#
# In `view right` the camera looks from +X: screen-left is her front.

reset
