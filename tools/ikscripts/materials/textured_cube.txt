# THE SPECULAR / GLOSSINESS BAKE on a spec/gloss glTF WITH MAPS (2026-11-09):
#   POSESTUDIO_TEST_FIGURE=<assets>/sg_textured.gltf tools/ikscripts/run.sh tools/ikscripts/materials/textured_cube.txt <out>
# (the asset from tools/materials/make_test_assets.py). The Mapped material carries a diffuse map
# (a red / blue checker) and a spec/gloss map (left half gold specular with gloss 0.9, right half
# 4 % grey with gloss 0.3): the decode layer bakes the solve per texel, so the zone ends with a
# base-color map, a metalness map and a roughness map, and its scalars at 1 — the maps carry
# everything. The Plain material has factors only.

expect matmap.roughness == 1

expect mat.roughness == 100
