# Poses Step by Step

Every pose below is made the same way: grab a part of the body and drag it where you want it. The recipes tell you *which* part to grab and *which way* to take it. Use the side view (`3`) for the up-and-down gestures, the front view (`1`) for sideways ones, and the Home view (`5`) for a three-quarter look; you can orbit at any time between drags. Every drag is one undo step, so experiment freely.

Distances assume a standard-size adult figure; a small character needs proportionally smaller drags.

## Reaching and leaning

- **Raise an arm**: drag the hand up. The elbow finds its own place; past shoulder height the trunk leans to help, and past what the arm gives she rises onto her toes.
- **Reach forward, out of reach**: drag the hand away. The body leans as far as its balance allows and stops there — a hand you cannot reach is left short, never with the figure falling over. Take the hips along if you want her to step after it.
- **Lean the body**: drag the chest sideways or back. A short pull leans; a longer one, and the feet step under her. Drag the head instead for the same lean carried through the neck.
- **Bow**: drag the chest (or the head) forward *and down*. She folds at the hips with the spine curling and the head staying up to look ahead, and she takes no step.
- **Hands on hips, elbows out**: drag the hand to the hip, pin it (`P`), then drag the elbow outward — it swivels about the pinned hand.
- **Turn the head**: click the head, press `T` and move the mouse left or right to turn it; `B` nods it and `S` tilts it sideways. Click to keep the result (a badge under the strip shows which of the three is on).

## Crouching, kneeling, sitting

- **Crouch**: drag the hips straight down. The knees bend, the heels lift when the ankles run out, and the arms hang. Drag them back up to stand.
- **Half squat / hips back**: drag the hips down and back. The trunk hinges forward over the feet to keep the balance.
- **Kneel**: drag the hips down and *forward* — the knees come down onto the floor once they are within a few centimetres of it, the feet lie flat on their tops behind her with the toes pointing back, and the trunk stays upright. Push lower than the thighs allow and she stops; to get lower, drag the hips **back** so she sits onto her heels.
- **Sit on the floor**: drag the hips down and back until the seat reaches the floor. From there, dragging the chest back **lays her down** on her back, and dragging it forward again sits her up; dragging a foot slides a leg out.
- **Sit on a chair**: drag the hips down (and back) to the seat's height, then **pin the hips** (`P`). Now she is held by the seat: lean the chest forward or back and the pelvis rocks like a seated person's, put a foot out, reach a hand. Balance and stepping stay off while the pin is set.
- **Stand up again**: from a kneel or a sit, drag the hips — or the chest, or the head — back up. The body rises, the legs straighten, and the feet come flat.

## On the floor

- **All fours**: kneel first (hips down and forward), let go, then drag the **chest forward and down** until the hands reach the floor. The arms hang plumb under the shoulders as the trunk tilts, and the hands land palms down. Push the chest lower still and the elbows bend into a push-up's bottom.
- **Kneel up from all fours**: drag the chest up and back over the hips. She comes up onto her knees with her arms hanging. From the kneel — sat back on the heels or tall — the hips or the chest stand her up: the knees let go once the hips are asked above a tall kneel.
- **Lie on her belly**: from all fours, drag the **hips forward and down**, a thigh's length and more ahead of the knees. The thighs go forward, the shins fold up behind, and she lies face down with the hands where they were. Turn the head afterwards: select it, press `T` and move the mouse, or use the Transform tab's Twist dial.
- **Get up off her belly**: drag the hips **up** — straight up, or up and back — and she comes up onto all fours. For a push-up's hand placement, first drag each hand forward along the floor to under its shoulder, then drag the hips up and a little back: she ends on all fours with the arms straight, the hips resting where the arms hold them. If the hips go further back than the straight arms allow, the hands let go of the floor over about half a second and the arms hang, the fingertips brushing the floor; drag the hands down to plant them again.
- **Half kneel** (one knee up, the other down): kneel, then drag one **knee up and forward** to hip height. Past what the shin allows over the lying foot, that foot lets go, hangs from the rising shin, turns and lands ahead under the knee, flat.
- **Lunge**: drag one foot back along the floor a stride and let go, then drag the hips down: the back knee comes toward the floor while the front knee bends over its foot.

## Walking and steps

- **Walk her across the floor**: drag the hips. The feet step under her, one after the other, and a last gathering step brings the trailing foot in when you stop. Walk at a natural pace; a very fast drag lets the walk finish after the cursor stops.
- **Step forward, back or sideways with one foot**: drag a foot along the floor. It slides, the sole staying flat, and the hips come between the feet.
- **Lift a foot**: drag the foot up. It is placed by its leg alone; the rest of the body stays. A lifted foot stays lifted through later drags of other joints — until a crouch brings it back to the floor.
- **Draw a knee up**: drag a knee upward from standing. The foot lets go and trails under the knee; bring the knee back down and the foot returns to its spot.

## Lifting her off the floor

- Pull a hand up steadily, beyond the arm's reach. After a quarter of a second she lifts off and hangs from the hand; keep pulling and the whole body rises. Lower the hand and she lands on her feet and stands back up where she came down. Pinned joints stay pinned through a lift.

## Fixing a pose

- **A limb went somewhere odd**: select the joint and use **Reset Limb**, or `Ctrl+Z`.
- **The feet came off the floor** (after turning joints with `B` / `S` / `T` or the Transform tab, or a loaded pose): press the **Ground** button.
- **Everything at once**: **Reset Pose**.
- **Left and right ended unequal** on a symmetric pose: pose one side, then **Mirror Limb to Other Side**.
