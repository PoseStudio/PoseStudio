What this is for
----------------

PoseStudio's render engine (Cycles, loaded as a plugin from the "engines/cycles" folder) can
render on NVIDIA graphics cards through CUDA and OptiX. To do so the engine is compiled against
NVIDIA's OptiX headers and its GPU kernels are compiled with NVIDIA's CUDA compiler. That
software is NVIDIA's, under NVIDIA's own license terms, and is not open source. The permission
above says that PoseStudio's license does not stand in the way of building, using and
distributing PoseStudio together with it.
